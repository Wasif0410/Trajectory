## Voice rules for entries

These are not style preferences, they are requirements. Anything writing an
entry should follow them.

1. **No em dashes.** Ever. Use a full stop, a comma, or brackets.
2. **Casual and human.** Write it the way you'd tell a friend what happened.
   Contractions are fine. Fragments are fine. Starting with "Nope." is fine.
3. **Lead with what actually happened**, not with a definition. "Spent an hour
   on this" beats "In modern web development, hydration is...".
4. **Keep it short.** Three or four short paragraphs is plenty.
5. **No filler openers.** Skip "In today's fast-paced world" and anything that
   sounds like it's introducing a blog post.
6. **Cool Facts stays non-technical.** If it could sit in the Professional
   folder, it belongs there instead.
