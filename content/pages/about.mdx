## Why this exists

This isn't a portfolio. I already have one of those.

This is where I put what I actually pick up during the day, before it
disappears. I kept solving the same problem twice because the first fix never
got written down anywhere I'd go looking for it. So now it goes into one of
three folders the same day I hit it.

Keeping it public isn't really for anyone else. I'm just a lot less likely to
skip a day when the gap is sitting there where I can see it.
