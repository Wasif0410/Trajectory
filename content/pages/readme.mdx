This isn't a portfolio. It's where I keep what I pick up.

Every day I file what I learned into one of three folders:

- **Professional**: work, coursework, whatever I'm building
- **Personal**: everything else. Habits, camera stuff, training, how I work
- **Cool Facts**: something I found out that I just liked knowing

## Getting around

Open **What I Learned** for the folder of days, pick a date, then pick a
folder. That opens the notes for that day straight away. **Daily Log** is
everything at once if you'd rather just scan a list.

Windows drag by the title bar, resize from the bottom right corner, and stack,
so you can pull a few days open next to each other. Anything already open comes
back to the front instead of opening a second copy.

## How it works

The folder counts, the day count and the streak all get read from the entry
files when the site builds. There's no number stored anywhere that I could set
by hand.

Everything here is written by me. Some days I write a few at once and space
them out, so the date on an entry is when it went up, not always the exact day
I worked it out.
