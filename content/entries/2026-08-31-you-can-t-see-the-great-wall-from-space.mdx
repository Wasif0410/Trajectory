---
date: 2026-08-31
title: "You can't see the Great Wall from space"
category: fact
tags: [space, history]
---

It's long, but it's only a few metres wide and roughly the same colour as the ground around it. Astronauts have said flat out that they couldn't pick it out.

Highways and airports are much easier to spot from up there. The wall just blends in.
