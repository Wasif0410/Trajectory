---
date: 2026-10-09
title: "Bananas are berries, strawberries aren't"
category: fact
tags: [nature, food]
---

Botanically, a berry is a fruit that grows from a single flower with one ovary, with the seeds embedded in the flesh. Bananas fit that.

Strawberries don't. The fruit grows from a flower with many ovaries, and the seeds sit on the outside.
