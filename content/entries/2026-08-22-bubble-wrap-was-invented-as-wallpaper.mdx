---
date: 2026-08-22
title: "Bubble wrap was invented as wallpaper"
category: fact
tags: [history, inventions]
---

Two engineers sealed a pair of shower curtains together in 1957 trying to make a textured wall covering. Nobody wanted it.

It flopped as wallpaper, got briefly pitched as greenhouse insulation, and only took off once someone thought to wrap fragile things in it.
