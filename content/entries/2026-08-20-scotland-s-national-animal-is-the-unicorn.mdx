title: "Scotland's national animal is the unicorn"
category: fact
tags: [history, animals]
---

Not a metaphor, not a tourism gimmick. The unicorn is Scotland's official heraldic animal and has been since the 1400s, when it turned up on the royal coat of arms.

It's still on the UK coat of arms today, standing opposite the English lion. And it's chained, which is a choice.
