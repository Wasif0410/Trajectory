title: "Wombat poop is cube shaped"
category: fact
tags: [animals, nature]
---

Wombats produce cube shaped droppings. Nothing else in the animal kingdom does this.

It comes down to the last stretch of their intestine, which has stiff and stretchy sections that squeeze the waste into flat sides as it passes through.

The shape is useful to them. Wombats mark territory by leaving droppings on rocks and logs, and cubes don't roll off.
