title: "bananas are slightly radioactive"
category: fact
tags: [food, science]
---

Bananas are high in potassium, and a small fraction of natural potassium is
potassium-40, which is radioactive.

The dose is so small it is used as a unit for explaining other doses. You
would need to eat several million in one sitting to have a problem.
