title: "A light bulb in California has been on since 1901"
category: fact
tags: [history, inventions]
---

It's in a fire station in Livermore and they call it the Centennial Bulb. Hand blown, thick carbon filament, left running almost continuously for over a century.

It has its own webcam, so you can go and check that it's still lit.
