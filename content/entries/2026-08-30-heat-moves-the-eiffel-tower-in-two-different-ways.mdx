title: "heat moves the Eiffel Tower in two different ways"
category: fact
tags: [engineering, france]
---

Heat expands the iron, so the tower can become a few millimetres taller. Uneven
sunlight also makes it lean, and the top can trace a circle about 15 centimetres
across.

The 15 centimetre figure is movement at the top, not extra height.
