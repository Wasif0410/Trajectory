title: "Australia lost a Prime Minister at the beach"
category: fact
tags: [history, australia]
---

Harold Holt went for a swim off the Victorian coast in 1967 and was never seen again. No body was ever found. Sitting Prime Minister, just gone.

Australia later named a public swimming pool after him, which is either a lovely tribute or the darkest joke a government has ever committed to.
