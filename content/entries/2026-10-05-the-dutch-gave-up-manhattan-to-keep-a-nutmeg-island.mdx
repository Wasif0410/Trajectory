title: "The Dutch gave up Manhattan to keep a nutmeg island"
category: fact
tags: [history, geography]
---

After a war with England in the 1660s, the two sides settled by swapping territory. England kept Manhattan. The Dutch kept Run, a tiny island in Indonesia.

Nutmeg was worth a fortune back then, so the Dutch thought they got the better deal.
