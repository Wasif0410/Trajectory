title: "Everest isn't the farthest point from Earth's centre"
category: fact
tags: [geography, nature]
---

Everest is the highest point above sea level, but the point farthest from the centre of the Earth is the summit of Chimborazo in Ecuador.

The Earth bulges at the equator, and Chimborazo sits almost on it, so its peak ends up farther out than Everest's.
