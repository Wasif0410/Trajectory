title: "Oxford University is older than the Aztec Empire"
category: fact
tags: [history, learning]

There was teaching happening at Oxford by 1096. Tenochtitlan, the Aztec capital, wasn't founded until 1325.

So Oxford had already been going for more than two centuries before the Aztecs laid their first stone. The place was old before the empire even started.
